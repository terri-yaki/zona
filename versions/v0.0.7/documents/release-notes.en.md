# Zona 0.0.7

## Your inbox is ready when you are

Recent alerts, sources, and settings open from your phone first, then quietly
catch up.

- **Open Zona and get moving.** Your recent inbox appears right away, so
  checking an alert feels effortless.
- **Useful even between connections.** Keep browsing recent alerts while
  offline, and Zona catches up when you reconnect.
- **Saved for you, and only you.** Each account stays separate, and its saved
  content leaves the phone when you sign out.
- **You stay in control.** See how much Zona has saved and clear it any time
  from Settings.
