# Zona 0.0.7

## Your inbox is ready when you are

Open Zona and your recent alerts are already waiting—even when the connection
isn’t.

- **Open and get moving.** Your recent alerts appear right away, so you can act
  without waiting on a loading screen.
- **Still useful offline.** Read recent alerts between connections, then let
  Zona quietly catch up when you’re back online.
- **Saved only for you.** Your saved inbox stays separate from every other
  account and disappears from the phone when you sign out.
- **You decide what stays.** See how much space Zona uses and clear saved
  content anytime from Settings.
