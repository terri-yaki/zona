# Zona adapts more smoothly

The app can now keep you informed when something changes, without getting in the way of essential controls.

## Helpful notices at the right moment

Zona can explain maintenance or important updates inside the app when they matter.

## Only the updates meant for you

What's New can show the right highlights for your phone and hide outdated cards without erasing history.

## Essential controls stay within reach

Even during a temporary service pause, privacy, account, and safety actions remain available.

## A steadier foundation

Zona can adjust selected features safely as the service grows, so fixes can reach you with less disruption.
