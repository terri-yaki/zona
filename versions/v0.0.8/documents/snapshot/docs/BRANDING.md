# Zona Notify branding

## Brand decision

The public product name is **Zona Notify**. In everyday use, the app is simply
**Zona**.

| Surface | Name or copy |
| --- | --- |
| App Store and Google Play | Zona Notify |
| Home Screen | Zona |
| In-app name | Zona |
| Premium plan | Zona Plus |
| Suggested web and social handle | `zonanotify` |
| English subtitle | Every PC alert, in your pocket |

Use **Zona Notify** where the product needs to be distinguished or discovered.
Use **Zona** in conversation and inside the product, where the context is
already clear.

## Wordmark

The preferred lockup is:

```text
ZONA
NOTIFY
```

`ZONA` is the brand. `NOTIFY` is a smaller descriptor, not an equal part of the
logo. The existing calm green palette and white bell remain the core visual
language. A notification pulse or signal can form a subtle Z shape so the icon
still reads clearly at small sizes.

## Product promise

Primary line:

> Every PC alert, in your pocket.

Supporting idea:

> Zona keeps every computer within reach.

The voice is calm, direct, and human. Describe what someone can do or feel;
avoid leading with APIs, databases, relays, payloads, or infrastructure.

## N-to-Z animation

The launch or promotional animation can use the proposed transformation
without establishing `Zotify` as a separate name:

1. `NOTIFY` appears.
2. A notification pulse strikes the `N`.
3. The `N` folds or rotates into a `Z`.
4. The remaining letters shrink into the smaller `NOTIFY` descriptor.
5. `ONA` unfolds beside the Z to form `ZONA`.
6. End on the Zona Notify lockup and “Every PC alert, in your pocket.”

App Store preview videos autoplay without sound, so the first transformation
must work visually and the final message must not depend on narration.

## Why not Zotify

Do not use **Zotify** as the product name or final campaign lockup.

- It sounds strongly associated with Spotify.
- An established Spotify-oriented downloader already uses the name.
- It creates search confusion unrelated to Zona's purpose.
- It adds avoidable trademark and mistaken-affiliation risk.

The N-to-Z motion remains useful as a visual idea; it should resolve to
**Zona Notify**, not stop at **Zotify**.

## Store metadata

Recommended English metadata:

- Name: `Zona Notify`
- Subtitle: `Every PC alert, in your pocket`

Apple currently limits both fields to 30 characters. The subtitle is exactly
30 characters. Keep the installed display name as `Zona`; it remains visibly
related to the store name and does not require changing the existing bundle
identifier.

Traditional Chinese metadata should translate the benefit naturally rather
than transliterating the name. Keep `Zona Notify` as the brand and localize the
subtitle when preparing the store listing.

## Before public monetization

App Store availability is not trademark clearance. Before purchasing a domain,
producing a large campaign, or launching Zona Plus publicly:

1. Reserve `Zona Notify` in App Store Connect and Google Play Console.
2. Check relevant trademark registers in the intended sales regions.
3. Reserve the preferred domain and social handles.
4. Check competing software and notification products, not only exact names.
5. Keep dated records of the chosen name, icon, and first commercial use.
