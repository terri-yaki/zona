# Zona 0.0.8

## Your Zona comes with you

Protect the Zona you have built, bring it to a new phone, and change a sender key without starting over.

- **Pick up on a new phone.** Protect your Zona, sign in again, and your sources and recent inbox are ready to meet you.

- **Sign in your way.** Choose email, Apple, Google, or GitHub, then add another method for extra peace of mind.

- **Change a key, keep everything else.** Give scripts and agents their own keys, replace one safely, and keep the same source name, sound, filters, and history.
